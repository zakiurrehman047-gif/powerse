Peptides are short chains of amino acids, the same building blocks that make up proteins. Research peptides are synthesized compounds supplied to laboratories for in-vitro and pre-clinical study, so researchers can examine how specific sequences behave under controlled conditions.

No. Every PWRUP product is sold strictly for laboratory research use only. They are not drugs, foods or cosmetics, are not intended to diagnose, treat or prevent any condition, and must not be administered to humans or animals.

Each lot is sent to an independent third-party lab. HPLC (high-performance liquid chromatography) confirms purity, and mass spectrometry confirms the molecular identity matches the expected sequence. Batches below our ≥99% purity standard are never released.

Yes. Every vial carries a lot code that matches a published Certificate of Analysis (COA). You can view the full HPLC chromatogram and MS report for that exact batch in our lab reports.

Lyophilized means freeze-dried. Water is removed under low temperature and vacuum, leaving a stable powder. This keeps the peptide far more stable in transit and storage than a liquid form, and every vial is then sealed under nitrogen.

Keep lyophilized vials sealed, dry and away from light. For long-term storage, −20 °C is recommended; 2–8 °C is suitable for the short term. Once reconstituted, keep solutions refrigerated and avoid repeated freeze-thaw cycles, which can degrade the peptide.

Orders ship within 24 hours on business days in insulated, cold-chain packaging with tracking. Each parcel is packed discreetly and includes the batch documentation for every compound inside.
